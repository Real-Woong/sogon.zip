Create a mobile app wireframe and UI design for a couple relationship app called Open Us.

## App Concept

Open Us is a couple app where two people can slowly reveal their preferences, feelings, and personal stories over time through “Open Cards.”  
The app also recommends date ideas based on both users’ private preferences, but private card contents are not shown to the partner until the owner confirms the reveal.

Core tagline:

“Today’s date is recommended. Our hearts open slowly.”

The design should feel warm, soft, emotional, private, and trustworthy.  
It should not feel like a typical dating app. It should feel like a private memory app for couples.

## Important Product Logic to Reflect in the UI

1. Each preference/story is saved as an individual Open Card.
2. Each Open Card has its own:
   - Tag
   - Content
   - Sensitivity
   - Open timing
   - Recommendation reflection ON/OFF
3. Private cards can still be used for recommendations.
4. The partner cannot see the private content until the owner confirms Open.
5. On the Open day, the owner receives a confirmation screen first.
6. Nothing is automatically revealed.
7. After Open, the partner can leave a reaction.
8. The Open moment is stamped into the couple calendar.
